# Workflow

Before fluorescence measurement, each sample or standard is mixed with the assay kit working solution. The prepared mixture is transferred into a disposable cuvette, which is then moved to the eviFluor Duo Fluorometer for measurement. The resulting fluorescence data are returned to the workflow for subsequent processing.

## Typical Sequence

1. Prepare standards and samples for the selected assay.
2. Aspirate 10 µl of the prepared solution into the pipette tip.
3. Pick up an eviEtte Micro Cuvette from the cuvette rack on top of the eviFluor Duo Fluorometer. The cuvette is picked up by inserting the pipette tip into the cuvette far enough to create a secure fit.
4. Move the cuvette to the measurement position of the eviFluor Duo Fluorometer and insert it into the cuvette guide.
5. Perform the fluorescence measurement.

The measurement order is standard high, standard low, followed by then samples.

## Measurement with or without air measurement

- With air measurement: The empty cuvette is measured before the sample measurement to account for autofluorescence of the cuvette. This workflow is recommended when **high measurement accuracy and precision are required**.
- Without air measurement: the workflow runs with sample measurements only after initialization.The sample is measured without a preceding empty-cuvette measurement. This workflow is **optimized for measurement speed and higher throughput**.
